Approach

How we build

  • Start from a real problem

    Every product begins with a problem people already have, studied before anything is built.

  • Design and build in-house

    We do the product design and the engineering ourselves, so each product is shaped end to end by the people who own it.

  • Honest about what is built

    We describe what our products do today. Anything still in progress is called planned.

  • Independent

    Gogon is self-funded, so the people who use our products are the ones we answer to.
