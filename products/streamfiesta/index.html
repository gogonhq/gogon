Streamfiesta

Cohort-based learning with social accountability. A Gogon product.

Not launched yet

The problem

Many people start online courses. Few finish them.

Streamfiesta gives learning a place in the week and people alongside you. Instructors run cohorts: groups that learn toward a shared goal between a start date and an end date. Learners take part in scheduled sessions, do work that gets real feedback, see how their progress is going, catch up when they fall behind, and finish with a recorded completion.

What it does

Built around finishing

  • Scheduled participation

    Each cohort has a schedule of lessons and live sessions. Sessions run on the instructor's own meeting link or in Streamfiesta's built-in classroom.

  • Meaningful work

    Checkpoints ask learners to show their work. Instructors approve it or ask for a revision with feedback. Peer feedback is available when the instructor allows it.

  • Visible progress

    A plain progress status shows whether a learner is on track, falling behind or finished. Smaller study groups share check-ins and progress.

  • Recovery

    When someone falls behind, Catch up lists concrete next actions: catching up on a missed session, planning time for unfinished work, or getting help from a peer.

  • Completion

    Completing every eligible requirement earns a recorded completion. Learners can choose to publish a proof of it under their name.

  • Tools for instructors

    Instructors create and publish cohorts, invite learners, teach with co-instructors, review work, and see who is waiting for an answer or falling behind.

Streamfiesta works in more than one language and for learners in different countries. A published proof is the learner's own record of finishing a cohort. It is not an accredited qualification.
